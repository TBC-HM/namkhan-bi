### 1.3 Conclusion of audit

The "far beyond 0%" is **analytics + catalogue plumbing + inventory recipes**, not operations:
- LIVE: revenue/capture analytics (folio + GL), catalogue CRUD in settings (empty data), recipe/deduction machinery.
- SCAFFOLDED, EMPTY, UNBRIDGED: `spa.*` operational schema (therapists, bookings).
- MISSING: rooms as a real resource, therapist display name, PostgREST bridges for `spa.*`, conflict-safe booking writes, any schedule/delivery UI, catalogue read surface for ops (CRUD hides in settings).
